# Code Of Conduct

Second Brain follows a simple rule: be respectful, practical, and privacy-aware.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Assume good intent.
- Keep discussions focused on the project.
- Respect user privacy and sensitive data.
- Use fake data in examples, issues, pull requests, and screenshots.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory language.
- Publishing another person's browser history or private data.
- Sharing tokens, credentials, private URLs, or personal records.
- Pressuring maintainers or contributors for unpaid urgent work.
- Derailing technical discussions with unrelated arguments.

## Privacy Standard

This project works with browsing history. Treat browsing history as private by default.

Do not ask people to share real DuckDB files, real browser databases, generated private vaults, or screenshots containing private URLs.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove comments, issues, pull requests, or contributions that violate this code of conduct.

Serious or repeated violations may lead to being blocked from participating in the project.
